Aquaponics: An Innovative System for Recycling Fish Industry Wastewater

PhD Seminar 2026

Comprehensive review of aquaponic systems as a circular, sustainable food production alternative. Covers bio-filtration & nitrogen cycling, water quality parameters, microbial hazards (Salmonella, E. coli, parasites, phages), One Health & antimicrobial resistance, smart monitoring via IoT/AI, system design models (DWC, NFT, Media Bed), and global regulatory frameworks.

🐓

Food & Feed Additives in the Poultry Sector

Co-authored · Hakan Şenarısoy

Comprehensive review on food and feed additives in the poultry industry: preservatives, antioxidants (BHA/BHT vs. natural), colorants (E-codes), emulsifiers & stabilizers, probiotics, phytobiotics (thyme, garlic, cinnamon), digestive enzymes, organic acids, amino acids, and mycotoxin binders. Covers EU, FDA, and Turkish regulatory frameworks and the shift away from antibiotic growth promoters.

Feed Additives Probiotics Phytobiotics AMR EU Regulations

📋 Food Safety Management Systems
📋

Food Safety Management Systems

Structured review of global FSMS — from HACCP's NASA origins (1960s) to today's standards. The 7 HACCP principles, ISO 22000 prerequisites (GAP/GMP/GHP/GLP/GDP), FSSC 22000 (2024 revision: food fraud, environmental monitoring, sustainability), BRCGS v9, IFS v7, SQF Edition 9, and GFSI benchmarking. Turkey's landscape: 2,100+ ISO 22000 certified businesses; ranked 3rd in Europe for FSSC 22000.
